# Data Provenance and Licensing

## 1. Source UI Strings (`data/source/en.txt`)
The 10,228 canonical English software user interface (UI) strings used in this benchmark originate from the K Desktop Environment (KDE) localization catalogs distributed via the OPUS KDE4v2 parallel corpus (Tiedemann, 2012; https://opus.nlpl.eu/datasets/KDE4). KDE software and its translation catalogs are distributed predominantly under the GNU General Public License (GPL) and GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL). Users reusing these strings should consult the KDE Licensing Policy (https://community.kde.org/Policies/Licensing_Policy) and OPUS terms of use.

## 2. Baseline and Self-Corrected LLM Translation Suites (`data/`)
The 120 localized UI translation suites (1,227,360 total segments across 10 suites and 12 target languages) and 46,818 diagnostic defect trace records were generated by the authors for scientific benchmarking and reproducibility under the respective model licenses and terms:
- **`Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` & `mistralai/Mistral-Nemo-Instruct-2407` outputs:** Produced locally under the Apache License 2.0.
- **`meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct` outputs:** Produced locally under the Meta Llama 3.1 Community License Agreement.
- **`google/gemma-2-9b-it` outputs:** Produced locally subject to the Google Gemma Terms of Use.
- **`gpt-4o-mini` outputs:** Generated via the OpenAI Chat Completions API and retained as a frozen research snapshot subject to OpenAI's Terms of Use.

## 3. Repository Code, Invariant Verifier, and Scripts
All original Python scripts, deterministic localization invariant verifiers, Invariant-Guided Self-Correction (IGSC) pipelines, evaluation utilities, and documentation authored for this study are released under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
